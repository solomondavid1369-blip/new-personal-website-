pumpkin spice oat milk

  • 1L oat milk, hot setting
  • 2 tbsp pumpkin purée
  • 1 tsp pumpkin spice
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup

Whisk it all into the warm milk. Drink it straight or pour it over a double shot.
